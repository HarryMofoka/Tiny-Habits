# 🎯 TinyHabits: Smart Habit Tracking with Adaptive Goals

## Transform Your Daily Habits with AI-Driven Goal Adaptation

TinyHabits is a modern habit-tracking application that uses intelligent goal adaptation to help you build lasting habits. Unlike traditional habit trackers that use rigid goals, TinyHabits adjusts to your performance, keeping you motivated and consistent.

![TinyHabits Dashboard Preview](/opengraph.svg)

## 🌟 Key Features

### 🤖 Intelligent Goal Adaptation
- **Smart Goal Adjustments**: Goals automatically adapt based on your performance
- **Growth-Focused**: Gradually increases challenges when you're succeeding
- **Struggle-Aware**: Gracefully reduces goals when you need support
- **Personalized Bounds**: Set min/max limits to keep goals realistic

### 📊 Visual Progress Tracking
- **Interactive Dashboard**: Real-time progress visualization
- **Streak Tracking**: Monitor your consistency
- **Trend Analysis**: Understand your habit patterns
- **Achievement Badges**: Celebrate your milestones

### 💪 Engagement Features
- **Quick Actions**: One-tap completion and tracking
- **Daily Notes**: Add context to your habit completions
- **Category Organization**: Group related habits
- **Progress Sharing**: Export and share your journey
